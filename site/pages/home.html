How a request is decided

From a doctor's request to an answer anyone can check

  1. 1

    It arrives

    Through the plan's prior authorization server, such as Perfuse, in the Da Vinci PAS standard, or keyed in from a fax, a call or the provider portal. Every route reaches the same engine.

  2. 2

    The evidence is found

    Vaglio reads the patient's record in the request and finds each piece of evidence the policy names, noting exactly where it came from.

  3. 3

    It decides, with proof

    The plan's signed policy is applied. The answer is approve, deny with its proof, or pend with the missing evidence named. Anything uncertain goes to a person.

  4. 4

    It is recorded

    The decision goes into a tamper-evident log before the answer is sent, with a receipt anyone holding it can check later.

What Vaglio has not done yet

We would rather you heard it here first.

The whole list {{ICON:arrow}}
  • No health plan runs it in production. Everything has been built and tested, on invented requests and against standard test kits, but no real patient's request has been decided by it.
  • It ships no licensed clinical criteria. InterQual and MCG are licensed products. Vaglio's library is drafted from public Medicare documents; a plan brings its own policies or its own licence.
  • Nobody outside BioDream has reviewed it. Its threat model, safety hazard log and HIPAA mapping are written down and published, but no independent auditor or clinician has signed them.
  • It is not certified. No SOC 2, no HITRUST, no penetration test by an outside firm, and no service levels: it is software a plan runs, not a service someone runs for it.
