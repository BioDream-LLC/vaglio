Section 7: Additional criteria for Rheumatoid Arthritis (RA) (All of the following criteria must be met)
1. Is the patient at least 18 years of age or older?
2. Does the patient have a diagnosis of moderately to severely active rheumatoid arthritis
demonstrated by description of baseline symptoms present in the chart notes?
3. Has the patient had an adequate trial and failure of at least ONE disease modifying antirheumatic
drug (DMARD) for at least 3 months (e.g. hydroxychloroquine, leflunomide, methotrexate,
sulfasalazine) or a contraindication to all, if clinically appropriate?
