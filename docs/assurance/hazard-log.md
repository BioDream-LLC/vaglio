# Hazard log: the decision logic

Ways Vaglio's decisions could harm a patient or mislead a plan, what in the design stands against each, and how that is
tested. In the manner of a clinical safety case (DCB0129-style), but written by Vaglio's developers and reviewed by no
clinical safety officer: a plan adopting Vaglio needs its own clinical safety review. The harm ratings are the
developers' judgement.

| # | Hazard | Harm | Controls in the design | Tested by | What remains |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | A request is denied because evidence is missing, not because it is against the policy | Care delayed or refused wrongly (high) | Missing evidence never denies: the engine issues a denial only if no combination of the unaddressed evidence would overturn it; otherwise it pends and asks for exactly that evidence. The check is exhaustive: a policy may declare at most 40 pieces of evidence (refused at parse otherwise); up to 16 every combination is solved, past that a search finds exactly the sets of unaddressed evidence that would change the outcome, and if that search runs out of budget the decision is cautious: a denial pends, asking for every piece not documented (`Result.Cautious`). A denial may rest on evidence documented absent (with its citation), never on evidence not addressed. This is enforced on every decision in `internal/decide`, whatever the policy says | `internal/decide` tests, including brute force over all 3^5 requests to the example policy; `FuzzRequest`; the search checked against solving every combination on every library policy and 400 random ones, and its cautious answer tested (`internal/decide/search_test.go`) | A policy author marking evidence present by default |
| H2 | A machine denies without a clinician | Denial without medical judgement; unlawful in some states (high) | `-clinician-denials`: the engine only proposes a denial; a clinician (NPI and credential recorded) decides it | `TestClinicianDenials` and the work-queue tests | The plan must turn it on where law requires |
| H3 | The policy encodes the coverage document wrongly | Every decision under it wrong (high) | Every clause is quoted word for word from its source; drafts are marked draft and decide nothing until a clinician signs them off; the checker finds undecided combinations and contradictions; replay shows what a new version would change on past cases | `internal/draft` gates; `vaglio readings`; `TestReplay`, `TestVersionsAndReplayCase` | A clinician signing off a draft without reading it against its source |
| H4 | A model invents or misreads evidence from a document | Approval or denial on evidence that is not there (high) | A model's reading is a proposal; each finding must quote the document, the quote gate refuses any it cannot find, and a reviewer confirms each. The gate checks that the words are there, not that they mean what the finding says: a true quote can be read out of its context, which is what the reviewer, with the document beside it, is for. With `-approve-from-documents` no reviewer sees an approval first: it is approve-only (a misread approval, never a misread denial), and sampled checks come after. Automatic approval from documents is opt-in, approve-only, sampled for checks | `reader.Gate` tests; `TestAutomaticReading`; the stand-in model tests | Real models' error rates are not measured |
| H5 | OCR or a transcription model misreads a fax | As H4 (high) | Text made by OCR or a model is marked as such on the case, goes through the same quote gate, and the reviewer confirms against the original, served beside it | `TestTesseractReadsAFax`, `TestPageReaders` | OCR errors in a quote that still matches the misread text |
| H6 | The wrong policy decides a request | Decided against criteria that do not apply (medium) | Precedence by authority (NCD over LCD or state over the plan's), line of business and state; ties refused; a lower policy may approve but never deny when a higher one may apply and the request does not say | `internal/precedence` tests | A request's coverage or address given wrongly |
| H7 | A decision is changed after the fact, or a different one shown | The record misleads an appeal or a regulator (high) | Hash-chained append-only log; the server refuses a broken chain; receipts let anyone holding one check it; with `-anchor-every`, signed checkpoints checked at every start and kept by outside witnesses, and receipts carrying inclusion proofs | `internal/audit`, `internal/receipt`, `internal/merkle` (RFC 6962 reference roots), `internal/anchor` (a log rewritten whole with every hash recomputed is caught); witnesses tried live: OpenTimestamps, Rekor, FreeTSA, DigiCert | Records made after the last checkpoint; a plan running no witness; anchoring is off by default |
| H8 | A decision is answered but not recorded | No record of what the provider was told (medium) | The request Bundle (and, since 2026-10-24, its directory entry) and then the record are synced to disk (fsync) before the answer is sent | The recovery drill: 0 answered decisions missing after `kill -9` under load. The power-cut test (`tools/powercut`, [recovery.md](recovery.md)): the disk's record replayed to every flush, 670 states on ext4 and 672 on XFS, each starting, verifying and deciding, and every decision answered before a mark found after it | The standby lags (H12); the power-cut test models a disk that keeps what it acknowledged: one that acknowledges a flush and then loses it (some consumer drives) defeats any software |
| H9 | A decision is late | CMS and state timeframes missed (medium) | Due dates on every pended case, expedited first; the queue orders by them; extensions recorded with their reason and limit | `internal/cases` timeframe tests; metrics report tests | Staffing |
| H10 | Errors fall harder on some patients | Unequal harm (medium) | The fairness report: reversal and pend rates by sex, age, race, ethnicity, state and line, with Fisher's exact test, small cells suppressed | `internal/fairness` tests | The report is read, and acted on, by people |
| H11 | A gold-carded provider is approved for a request a policy would deny | Approval without review (low: the plan chose the exemption) | The exemption cites its program and the provider's record; the engine's own result is kept beside it | `TestGoldCard` | |
| H12 | A primary is lost, and decisions answered just before it are not on the standby | Decisions given but not on the new primary's record (medium) | The standby follows the log continuously; with `-standby-sync` every answer waits until the standby holds it, except while the standby is down, when answers go out unconfirmed (recorded as `standby.unconfirmed`) and this hazard is back | `tools/drill` in CI (asynchronous); `TestStandbySync`; `tools/drill -standby-sync 2s`: 0 of ~1,635 lost in 3 runs | Asynchronous by default; synchronous mode falls back to asynchronous (recorded) while the standby is down |
| H13 | A shadow pilot's decision reaches someone | A provider acts on a decision the plan never made (high) | The shadow server answers 404 to every request; letters, webhooks, advice, eligibility, NCPDP, the portal and intake are off; serve refuses notices and webhooks beside it; shadow data never opens as live data | `TestShadowPilot`, `TestShadowDataKeptApart`; tried with Perfuse v0.1.16 | A PAS server not keeping to the decision service protocol |
| H14 | CQL (a DTR pre-fill) reads the record wrongly | Evidence found present or absent that the record does not show (high) | `find cql` is evidence like any other: a null, an error, or anything but true or false is not addressed, which never denies; the finding cites the define and the resources it read; with the questionnaire's own answer read beside it, a disagreement is a contradiction a reviewer settles; the engine refuses what it does not do rather than guessing; libraries are pinned by SHA-256 | `internal/cql` tests; compared define by define with the reference CQL-to-ELM translator and `cql-execution` on three real DTR libraries (79 defines, two invented patients, all matching); `internal/cqlrun`, `TestCQLEvidence`; `FuzzCompile` | The engine implements part of CQL; value sets come only as given; dates read from FHIR text by shape |
| H15 | A library policy decides that no clinician of the plan has approved | Decisions against criteria the plan never accepted (medium) | `-require-adoption`: a library policy decides only once the plan's clinicians adopt its exact text (NPI and credential recorded); a new release's text waits for adoption while the adopted one keeps deciding; yearly review with warnings; the CMS source watch flags newer LCD/NCD versions | `TestAdoption`, `TestAdoptedVersionKeepsDeciding`, `TestRegistry`, `TestCompare` | On by default; `-require-adoption=false` (a demonstration, a trial) turns it off; the clinicians themselves |
| H16 | A decision in the plan's case-review system is attributed wrongly, or bypasses the rules | A denial without a clinician, or under another's name (high) | The decision comes back through the link's own token, is held to the console's rules, is recorded under `um:NAME` with the reviewer and the reference in the other system; a linked case cannot also be decided in the console | `TestCaseReviewSystem`; the stand-in UM server's tests | Not tried against a real UM system; the other system's own identity of its reviewers |
